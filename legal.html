Legal & Compliance

This page summarizes how Avexla handles privacy, service terms, HIPAA obligations, and security. For a full copy of any policy, contact our team.

Privacy Policy

Avexla collects only the information needed to respond to inquiries and deliver contracted services. We do not sell personal information. Contact form submissions are used solely to reply to your request and are retained only as long as necessary for that purpose.

Terms of Service

Use of this website is provided as-is for informational purposes. Engagements for professional services are governed by a separate written agreement between Avexla and the client, which takes precedence over any content on this site.

HIPAA Compliance

When Avexla handles protected health information on behalf of a covered entity, we operate under a Business Associate Agreement and apply administrative, physical, and technical safeguards consistent with the HIPAA Security Rule.

Security

Avexla applies zero-trust principles, encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access, continuous monitoring, and a documented incident response process across the systems we manage. To report a security concern, email our team.
